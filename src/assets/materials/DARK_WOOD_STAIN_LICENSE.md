# Dark Wood Stain PBR

Source: https://freepbr.com/product/dark-wood-stain-pbr/

Creator: FreePBR / Brian

The free download is licensed for non-commercial use in games and 3D work. It
must not be redistributed as a standalone texture set. Commercial use requires
a commercial-use purchase from FreePBR.

These game-ready derivatives use 2048x2048 textures. The 4K source albedo was
resized to 2K and encoded as a high-quality WebP, while the source-resolution
normal map and packed surface map are lossless WebP. The surface texture packs
ambient occlusion in red, roughness in green, and metallic in blue. The source
height map is not sampled alongside the normal map because both describe the
same small-scale surface relief.
