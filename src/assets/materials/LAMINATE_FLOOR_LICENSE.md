# Laminate Flooring Brown PBR

Source: https://freepbr.com/product/laminate-flooring-pbr-brown/

Creator: FreePBR / Brian

The free download is licensed for non-commercial use in games and 3D work. It
must not be redistributed as a standalone texture set. Commercial use requires
a commercial-use purchase from FreePBR.

These game-ready derivatives retain the source's 2048x2048 resolution. The albedo is a
high-quality WebP, while the normal map and packed surface map are lossless
WebP. The surface texture packs ambient occlusion in red, roughness in green,
and the source's uniform non-metallic value in blue.
